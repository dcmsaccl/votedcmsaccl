  • Sharp, well-lit, no glare

    Good to know

    Who can take part?

    Students in semester 8 to 10 and interns.

    How often do I submit?

    As often as you like, in Operative, Endo or Fixed.

    How are cases graded?

    Faculty score isolation and photo quality, then rank the cases. Rank 1 to 5 earns 10, 8, 6, 4 or 2 points.

    What do we win?

    Prizes and certificates at the end of the season. Top 3 are featured every week.

    How does voting work?

    Tap the heart on any case you like. Each device gets one vote per case, and you can tap the tooth again to undo your vote. Votes add up for the student and the leaderboard updates straight away.

    Can I delete a submission?

    Yes. Use the Delete my case button on your card. It only shows on the device you submitted from. If you changed device or cleared your browser, ask the faculty or the Dental Committee to remove it for you.
